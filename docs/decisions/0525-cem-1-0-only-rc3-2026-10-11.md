# Decision 0525: rc.3 ships `cem/1.0` as the only CEM; 0.x is removed and committed maps migrate

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: accepted as the rc.3 plan (owner instruction in chat 2026-10-11: "the
plan on RC3 is to make CEM 1.0 the only CEM being used. 0.1/2/3 will be removed and all legacy
CEMS should be migrated"). Amends decisions 0521 and 0523 and supersedes decision 0357's freeze
for rc.3. Every requirement ID named below is proposed; this decision accepts none of them.

## Context

At `origin/main` f0d0e6106 the product writes, reads and publishes three legacy wire versions
beside the experimental stable profile:

- Every one of the 593 committed maps under `.corvint/changes/` is `cem/0.2`. The dogfood loop
  refuses `cem/1.0` (`internal/dogfoodflow/change.go`, `CEM-V1-032`) and admits only
  `wire.Canonical` maps in batches (`internal/dogfoodflow/batch.go`).
- `corvint cem prepare` writes 0.2, `cem begin` writes 0.1, and `cover`/`discriminate`/structural
  `mark` upgrade to 0.3 (`internal/cem/workflow`). OCM `prepare` accepts only 0.1 or 0.2
  (`internal/lrfrepo/ocm_write.go`).
- `protocol/cem-1.0/stable/MIGRATION.json` lists 19 consumer families C01..C19, all
  `OPEN_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.
- The stable packet inherits 0.x text that is digest-pinned in its manifest:
  `protocol/cem-1.0/stable/inherited/` carries `cem-0.1-algorithms.md`, `cem-0.3.schema.json`
  and the 0.3 `ALGORITHMS.md` (CEM-SM-001..010). The `go1.27.2` structural tuple is
  `EXPERIMENTAL_TUPLE_PENDING_FULL_CORPUS` (`CEM-V1-050`, decision 0522).
- `interop/cem01-go` is the independent portable consumer for 0.1, 0.3, the candidate and the
  stable profile. Decision 0523 P0 adds its stable citation vectors there.
- No 0.x → 1.0 map converter exists. `AssembleStable` (`internal/cemcandidate/stable.go`) needs
  Tasks, runner and receipt inputs that historical changes never had.
- Of the 593 bind commits, 35 are not reachable from `origin/main` (squash/rebase merges before
  decision 0319). Three maps under `conformance/use-cases-v0/receipts/*/beamfall-dogfood/` pin
  commits that are absent from this repository.

Decisions 0521 and 0523 kept the 0.x wire bytes frozen beside 1.0 (`V1-0595` and `V1-0632`
acceptance; decision 0357; `CEM-CB-025`) and reduced the V1-0632 consumer migration to
`verify`/`status`/`report`. The owner instruction replaces that plan.

## Decision

### 1. What "only CEM" means

When rc.3 is cut, every Corvint producer writes `cem/1.0`, every consumer reads `cem/1.0`, and no
command, MCP tool, projection, gate or CI job reads or writes `cem/0.1`, `cem/0.2` or `cem/0.3`.
Every map committed in this repository is a `cem/1.0` map.

"Removed" means removed from the product source, the published `protocol/**` packets for 0.x,
the 0.x schemas and specs as governing contracts, the 0.x tests, fixtures and gates. Git history
keeps all of it. Released rc.1/rc.2 binaries keep their behavior. Removing Apache-2.0 text from
`protocol/**` withdraws no right already granted (`LICENSING.md`).

It does not remove the algorithms 1.0 inherits. The 0.1 algorithms, the 0.3 hunk vocabulary
and structural reasons (`CEM-SM-001..010`) and the 0.3 schema fragments the stable packet pins are
re-homed as `cem/1.0`-normative text and requirements. Their requirement IDs keep their meaning.
`interop/cem01-go` stays as the portable 1.0 consumer; only its 0.x paths go. A rename is out of
scope for rc.3.

### 2. Ownership

The "CEM 1.0 groundbreaking vision" session keeps the wire. It owns V1-1262, V1-1273, V1-1263,
V1-1264, V1-1265 and V1-1266, including the attest/anchor stable routing.

The orchestrating session owns these lanes:

- the converter
- the migration run
- the dogfood producer cutover
- the remaining consumer cutover (C01..C19 minus attest/anchor)
- the 0.x removal
- decision 0523 P3 (V1-1258) and P4 (V1-1261)

The wire owner reviews the converter against the frozen shape.

### 3. Order

1. V1-1262: consumer-computed tiers. Its pre-migration baseline over the committed `cem/0.2`
   maps is retained as the reference measurement.
2. V1-1273: regeneration tooling for the sealed stable result packet.
3. V1-1263 and V1-1264, then V1-1265, which freezes the stable result shape. Before that freeze,
   the wire owner does two things with the V1-1273 tooling:
   - re-homes the inherited 0.1/0.3 text (§1), which is digest-pinned in the stable manifest;
   - adds to the stable schema the provenance member and the closed list of producer-only
     members (§4).
4. Converter (§4) against the frozen shape, reviewed by the wire owner.
5. Consumer cutover, and the dogfood producer cutover with P3's reasoned `unknown` hunks. These
   may run in parallel with steps 1–4 where they do not depend on the frozen shape. V1-1266's
   "next 30 dogfood changes" criterion depends on the producer cutover. The producer cutover
   (V1-1276) depends on V1-1281: the V1-1262 baseline shows 171 of the 180 invariant citation
   blocks are the loop's own bind/seal maps citing one span for every hunk, and 45 maps have
   negative specificity, so the cutover emits one citation per hunk and marks the chore bind/seal
   maps as their own class before it writes `cem/1.0`.
6. Migration run: convert every committed map. Both the native consumer and `interop/cem01-go`
   re-verify every migrated map. Repin the use-case receipts. Re-run the V1-1262 report over the
   migrated maps beside the reference baseline.
7. 0.x removal, only after step 6 passes.
8. V1-1283, after steps 3 and 6: a corvint-tasks gate consumes the sealed `cem/1.0` verdict
   (`verdictSha256` and the consumer-computed citation block) as gate evidence. Tasks consumes
   no CEM today, and the provider-neutral criterion of decision 0523 P1 makes the native ticket
   binding optional, so without this step a ticket's native completion and the change's sealed
   verdict stay two unconnected proofs of the same change. Tasks never raises a tier and completion
   authority stays with the native store. This step is not an rc.3 kill criterion.

V1-0614 is re-scoped to the full-corpus structural qualification under `protocol/cem-1.0`. It
publishes no `protocol/cem-0.3` packet.

### 4. Converter contract

- It is a pure function of the 0.x map bytes and the Git objects those bytes pin. Running it
  twice gives identical bytes.
- It never changes a verdict. A hunk's disposition, bases and evidence carry over unchanged.
  Support tiers are computed by the consumer (decision 0523 P0), not by the converter.
- Members only a producer run could supply are emitted empty, never synthesized. These include
  criteria, receipts, artifacts, authentication, intent and scope, and structural witnesses.
  The wire owner adds the provenance member and the closed list of producer-only members
  (emitted empty) to the stable schema in V1-1265; the converter lane fills them.
- Every migrated map carries a provenance member naming the source spec, the source blob OID and
  SHA-256, the bind commit and the converter version. That keeps each migrated map pinned to the
  immutable Git content it came from (product invariant 1).
- A map whose pinned objects are unavailable converts from its bytes, then verifies to an
  explicit unavailable or abstaining result, never to an invented one (invariant 2). This covers
  the three Beamfall receipt maps, and any of the 35 unreachable bind commits a fresh clone lacks.
- A map the consumer cannot verify before conversion still converts from its bytes. One such map
  is `f0fda542` (877 hunks), which fails `git-budget-exceeded` on origin/main today. Both
  consumers must report the same pre-existing result for the migrated map, and that result does
  not count as a conversion difference.

### 5. Sealed-map rule

The migration rewrites each `.corvint/changes/<bind>.cem.json` in place, in one migration commit.
The file name stays the bind commit, and the provenance member names the 0.2 blob that commit
holds.

The migration PR itself enforces the one-transition rule. Its check re-runs the converter over
each provenance-named source blob and requires byte equality with the committed file. That run
is retained as evidence.

The migration commit then becomes the new seal baseline. From then on, `sealed-cem-modified` and
`sealed-cem-drift` compare against the migration commit's blobs and refuse every change. No 0.x
reader is needed after step 7, so the converter leaves with the rest of 0.x.

## Kill criteria

- The migration run stops if either consumer verifies a migrated map differently from its 0.x
  source, other than where a §4 empty member explains the difference.
- It also stops if a migrated map's `citation` block differs from its block in the retained
  pre-migration baseline, again unless a §4 empty member explains it. The baseline is
  `https://github.com/beamfall/corvint-history/blob/1c4d8793cf71cbb01b8509b68a6cb1502a40a13e/docs/build-log/2026-10-12-cem-citation-baseline-593-maps.md` (V1-1262). Tiers are
  consumer-computed, so they must be identical.
- When either stop condition fires, the difference is recorded and the 0.x removal waits.
- If V1-1265 does not land in time for rc.3, rc.3 is cut without the removal step. 0.x then stays
  as a recorded limit, and this decision's §1 is reported as unmet, not relabelled.

## Rollback

Revert this file and its README row. Decisions 0521 and 0523 then govern rc.3 again. Lanes
merged under this decision are reverted by their own merge commits in reverse order. A reverted
migration commit restores the 0.x maps byte for byte from Git. Tickets filed under this
decision stay in the queue with this decision named as their source. Nothing is tagged,
published, frozen or written to the ticket store by this decision itself.
