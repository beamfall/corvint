# Decision 0521: CEM 1.0 moves into the rc.3 scope

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: accepted (owner instruction in chat 2026-10-11: "I also want to get CEM
1.0 completed for RC3"). Amends decision 0491 §4.

## Context

Decision 0491 deferred the CEM 1.0 runner epic to 1.0.0 or later. On 2026-10-11 the owner asked for
CEM 1.0 to be completed for rc.3. Like decision 0491, this is a scope decision only. It adds,
amends and accepts no requirements, and every ticket keeps its own acceptance criteria and
completion evidence in the Corvint Tasks store.

## Decision

1. Decision 0491 §4 is amended. These items move from "deferred to 1.0.0 or later" into the
   `1.0.0-rc.3` scope:
   - the CEM 1.0 runner epic: V1-0591..V1-0596, V1-0614 and V1-0632;
   - its dependencies: V1-0612, V1-0620, V1-0622 and V1-0575.

   The other items in §4 stay deferred.
2. The following stay explicit blockers. Inclusion in scope does not satisfy them and implies none
   of them:
   - independent external consumer conformance (`CEM-V1-008`);
   - explicit owner promotion and acceptance of the stable CEM profile;
   - the Linux, Windows, Android and iOS device tuples.
   A CEM 1.0 item that cannot close one of these blockers ships in rc.3 as a recorded limit. It is
   not reported as qualified.
3. Requirements that lanes propose under this scope, such as `CEM-SM-011`..`CEM-SM-017` for the
   portable `protocol/cem-0.3` packet (V1-0614), keep their own acceptance path. This decision
   accepts none of them.
4. Publishing the `v1.0.0-rc.3` tag and release still needs separate explicit owner approval under
   `docs/RELEASE-RUNBOOK.md` and decisions 0486 and 0491 §5.

## Amendment 2026-10-11: native/portable divergence disposition

Source: the coordinator instruction on the V1-0614 lane, 2026-10-11. This amendment disposes of
the 17 divergences the first `protocol/cem-0.3` packet declared. It accepts no requirement:
`CEM-SM-011`..`CEM-SM-018` stay proposed. It changes no frozen packet and no `cem/0.1` or
`cem/0.2` byte.

1. `CEM-SM-007` is strict. An unchanged comment that still names `from` is not a proven rename,
   and neither is a partial rename that leaves an occurrence of `from`. Native `identSubstitution`
   is fixed. It is shared with the native stable verifier, so stable rename proofs become strict
   too; this narrows acceptance and widens no refusal into an accept.
2. Where native was laxer than the text, native is fixed, scoped to the new single-profile reader
   (`CEM-SM-018`, `ReadOptions.SingleSpec`): `spec-0.2` and `argument-wire-precedes-missing-base`
   are `unsupported-spec`, and `argument-uppercase-target` and `argument-width-mismatch` are
   `invalid-arguments`. The multi-version reader and CLI are unchanged.
3. Where the two differed only in code name, placement or precedence, `ALGORITHMS.md` already
   names the rule: retain native exit class and code/issue placement. Portable `verify-03` adopts
   the native result for `hunk-missing` (`uncited-hunk`), `evidence-uncited` (wire
   `orphan-evidence`), `evidence-duplicate` and `basis-duplicate-pair` (wire `invalid-field`),
   the three non-object JSON roots (`not-an-object`), `hunk-range-start-zero` (verification
   `invalid-field`), `hunk-supported-without-basis` (wire `unsupported-without-basis`),
   `evidence-span-empty` (`evidence-unavailable`) and `mode-change-executable-bit`
   (`malformed-patch`). `uncited-hunk` and `unsupported-without-basis` are also the names the
   frozen 0.1 packet uses. Every change keeps a refusal a refusal.
4. `envelope-config-section` leaves the packet. `primary-clean-config-bounded/1` is a declared
   portable implementation ceiling, and the algorithm text says exceeding one is operational
   unsupported, not a protocol rule. A portable-only test keeps the refusal.
5. The packet adds `json-at-map-limit`, a map of exactly 4 MiB that accepts. The packet stays on
   `go1.27.1`: retargeting it to `go1.27.2` touches the `run03` runtime gate that the Go pin
   change also edits, so it waits for that change.

Result: native and portable agree exactly on all 211 cases, and the native test admits no
per-case divergence.

Review follow-up, same day: the packet now carries `WP1-OBSERVED.json`, pinned by SHA-256, so a
consumer can derive the canonical patch from the packet alone. The escaped duplicate-key vector
now spells its duplicate as `"\u0073pec"`. Ten survivor-text and detail byte and character
boundary cases are added. Native and portable agree exactly on all 221 cases.

## Rollback

Revert this file and its README row. Decision 0491 §4 then defers the listed items again. Reverting
the amendment alone means reverting its commit, which restores the declared divergences. Nothing is
tagged, published or written to the ticket store by this decision.
