# Decision 0392 — accept `OCM-V0-016`

Date: 2026-09-25. Status: accepted. Authority: repository owner instruction "accept OCM-V0-016"
(2026-09-25). Ticket: V1-0273.

V1-0273 (PR #213) proposed that a verified `OCM-V0-013` dogfood aggregate with no linked requirement
say so, instead of reading as a clean `ready-for-review`.

`OCM-V0-016` is accepted as written (`docs/specs/ocm-v0-dogfood.md`). When the aggregate verifies
and `coverage.linked` is 0, `aggregate.findings` carries the one finding `no-requirements-linked`
with the message `0 of N declared requirements are linked to the change`. The finding leaves `state`
`ready-for-review` and grants or removes no authority. When at least one requirement is linked,
`aggregate.findings` is absent and the aggregate bytes are unchanged. The `ocm/0.1-experimental`
wire and the `ocm status` envelope are unchanged. The rest of the OCM spec keeps its proposed
status.

The acceptance evidence is the V1-0273 BUILD-LOG entry and
`internal/dogfoodocm/aggregate_test.go:TestAggregateFindsNoLinkedRequirements`.

Rollback: remove `linkageFindings` from `internal/dogfoodocm/aggregate.go` and restore the proposed
status of `OCM-V0-016`. No persisted format changes: `aggregate.findings` is an optional member
that only zero-linkage aggregates carry.
