## Risks carried forward

- `release readiness` reports only the missing candidate until one is captured, so it did not list
  the six OPEN v0-8 tickets. `release candidate` binds each scoped ticket's record digest without
  checking its status, and promotion requires every bound ticket COMPLETED with that digest
  (corvint-tasks `internal/store/release.go` and `release.Assess`). Close every scoped ticket before
  capturing a candidate; completing one afterwards changes its digest and needs a new capture.
- The two unchanged-candidate runs in V1-0020 run on a quiet host; `cmd/corvint` alone takes about
  591 s and fails under load (V1-0226).
- Any fix after V1-0019 unseals invalidates the candidate and restarts from step 7.
