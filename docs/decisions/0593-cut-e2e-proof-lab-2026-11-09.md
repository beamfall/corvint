## Context

The lab compiled change-specific browser scenarios, accepted and falsified Playwright tests,
minimized suites and answered "how do I X" from mined page objects. It shipped as four binaries
(`corvint-test`, `corvint-tests-accept`, `corvint-behavior-falsify`,
`corvint-playwright-minimize`) and eight internal packages, all experimental, with no adopter
evidence and real browser evidence `NOT_OBSERVED`. It carried its own CI job (`cce-live`), test
read scopes, shard costs and catalog entries, and kept `corvint-test-runner` folded as a shim.
None of it serves test selection for a diff.

## Decision

1. Delete `cmd/corvint-test`, `cmd/corvint-tests-accept`, `cmd/corvint-behavior-falsify`,
   `cmd/corvint-playwright-minimize` and the packages `internal/behaviorfalsify`,
   `internal/changee2e`, `internal/extmutate`, `internal/playwrightminimize`,
   `internal/skhproof` and `internal/testacceptance`, with their catalog trees, guides
   (`docs/E2E-ADOPTION.md`, `docs/EXTERNAL-APP-ACCEPTANCE.md`) and configuration entries.
2. `corvint-test-runner` stands alone again: its tree is authored, it has no `worker` entry, and
   `CTF-V0-010`'s guard (`TestCTFV0007CoreLinksNoFoldedGroup`) keeps core from linking
   `internal/testrunner/cli`.
3. `corvint affected audit` drops its `e2e` selector; an `e2e` input member is refused as an
   unknown member (`unsupported-affected-audit`, exit 2).
4. The application-map adoption workflow ends at `flows appmap build`. The TypeScript language
   plugin declares Mutation absent and Ruby's comes only from `rbmutate`.
5. The `.github` changes (the `cce-live` job and the `.github/cishards` lab entries) go in a
   separate change, because `github-split-check` (`ASD-V0-706`) refuses a range that mixes
   `.github` with code. Until then `make cce-live` is a no-op.

## Rollback

Revert the change. Nothing persisted or published depends on the deleted binaries; old CEMs and
build-log entries that name them stay readable as history.
