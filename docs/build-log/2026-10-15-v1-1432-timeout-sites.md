Item (4): a failed post-run observation that yields `STALE`/`UNKNOWN` currency is the intended
fail-closed outcome. `GLTP-V0-021` defines currency as recomputable from pre- and post-observation
digests: "an absent post-observation yields `UNKNOWN`", and drift on either axis forces execution
`INCOMPLETE` with failure class `STALE`, never `PASSED` or `FAILED`. A deadline-only failure leaves
the post-observation absent. The run cannot prove its source or toolchain stayed current, so it
must not display as current. `STALE` there means "currency not proven", not "drift observed". A
reader who needs the deadline cause reads the authority error, which `GLTP-V0-054` already reports
as `post-run authority deadline expired`.
