## Design and deliberate conservatism

Declarations and parameter references are retained in every scope, including opaque inline
shells and make recipes. Parsed uses in commands, operands and redirects keep local values
computed. Command references remain separate, including interpreters, source operands, traps,
`env -S` and feeder commands. No local value becomes a path edge. The raw occurrence counter
and function/arithmetic detection regexes are removed.

Continuations are joined before keyword/name recognition, and escaped names are decoded by the
word scanner. Narrowing still abstains on these spellings, quoted/escaped names, functions,
loops, control flow, wrappers, incomplete parsing, source/eval, arithmetic/indirection and opaque
scripts. Here-doc bodies containing `$(`, backticks or `${` retain a computed fallback even with
no referenced local; ordinary parameter references in those bodies also count as uses.

Two existing positive cases deliberately change: `if ok=1; then :; fi` and
`for f in a b; do :; done` now stay computed. Other existing AFP-V0-9621/9622/9623/9626/9628
expectations remain intact. The root-like exemption for commands without any local declaration
and the documented runner-environment assumption remain outside this redesign.

AFP-V0-9628 moves from `affected-zero-unknown-v0.md` to the requested
`affected-plan-selection-v0.md`, retaining its ID and amending its contract. AFP-V0-9622/9624
are aligned, and `make spec-requirements` regenerates the requirement index and README.
