## V1-3716: completion health in `receipt audit` (TCD-V0-680)

Criterion 1 is delivered. `receipt audit` adds two members:
- `completionsByDay`: VERIFIED and MANUAL completions by UTC day, newest first, at most 31 rows.
- `completionHealth`: `OK`, `NO_VERIFIED` or `NO_COMPLETIONS`, over the 7 days ending on the newest
  completion day. `NO_VERIFIED` adds a warning.

The window is anchored on the store rather than the clock, so the read is deterministic. The code
lives in `internal/tasks/cli/receipt_health.go` plus a few lines in `receiptAudit` after
`completions`. Those lines fall outside the `--through` hunks in PR #46; a trial merge-tree
auto-merged `receipt.go`. tkg-w20 does not touch `receipt.go`. The `receipt audit` help note in
`command_help.go` was not changed, because tkg-w20 is editing that file. Add the two members to the
note after tkg-w20 lands.

Criteria 2 and 3 are a policy fork and are not built. They need new keys in two closed persisted
profiles: `statusChangedAt` in `taskman-ticket/0` and the receipt operation name in
`taskman-receipt/0`.
- Option A: open `taskman-ticket/1` and `taskman-receipt/2`, with readers that accept both versions
  before any writer emits the new ones (two-step rollout). Old records stay UNKNOWN.
- Option B: derive both values on read from the journal. A status change is the receipt that
  changed `status`, and the operation is the receipt's request verb. No profile changes. The cost is
  one journal walk, which `receipt audit` already performs.
- Option C: leave both as UNKNOWN and rely on criterion 1.

Recommendation: B. It needs no closed-profile revision, it covers history, and it can later be
promoted to A if derivation proves too slow. The owner decides.
