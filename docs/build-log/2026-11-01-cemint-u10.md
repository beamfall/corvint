## V1-3822: lane-scripts_test.sh group 7 under the lane cache env

Cause: the fixture's fake ssh ran the remote script with the caller's environment minus `GOCACHE`
only. heavy.sh's remote side (`: "${CORVINT_GOCACHE:=...}"; : "${GOCACHE:=$CORVINT_GOCACHE}"`)

was a fixture-fidelity bug, not a heavy.sh bug. Decision: the fake ssh also drops
`CORVINT_GOCACHE`. Evidence: 40 groups pass with and without the lane env
(`CORVINT_GOCACHE=GOCACHE=$HOME/.cache/go-build-cemint`), and group 21 passed on this host too.
