  `internal/goplsclient` (the editor companion) negotiated. The provider now offers UTF-16 only,
  refuses any other or malformed `positionEncoding`, queries only the advertised `definition` and

  `position_encoding`, `methods`, `unavailable_methods` and `executable_sha256` in the query
  summary. The digest is observed, not authenticated, and is bounded at 1 GiB; an unreadable
  executable fails as `gopls executable unreadable`. `TestExpandNegotiatesBeforeQuerying_EEPV0821`
  covers default, definition-only, references-options-only, utf-8 refused, null refused and no
  providers refused, and checks that no textDocument query follows a refusal.

  `internal/lspstdio/semantic_test.go` now runs on every semantic harness initialize and fails on
  any standard capability outside `positionEncoding`, `textDocumentSync` and `experimental`, or a
  `textDocumentSync` member outside `openClose`, `change` and `save`. A falsifier that added
  `hoverProvider` to the initialize result failed `TestSemanticOverlayDefinition` with
  `untested standard capability "hoverProvider" advertised`, and the change was reverted.
