Method: replay the 26 changes in `benchmarks/selection-baseline/changes.json`. For each, run the
binary built at 900444b13 code with `affected --no-cache --base BASE`, in a detached worktree at
HEAD. Fast tier means AFP-V0-162 tier 0 or 1. Reader-only witnesses are UNBOUNDED_READER,
PATH_LITERAL_READER and DECLARED_READ_SCOPE.

  - PATH_LITERAL_READER 1,128
  - UNBOUNDED_READER 357
  - DECLARED_READ_SCOPE 104
- Fast tier: 1,428 selected units, of which 68 are reader-only (4.8%):
  - PATH_LITERAL_READER 53
  - UNBOUNDED_READER 11
  - DECLARED_READ_SCOPE 4
  - The other fast-tier units are DEPENDENCY_PATH 992, DIRECT_SOURCE_CHANGE 184, DIRECT_TEST_CHANGE 130, ENCLOSING_PACKAGE 35 and BINARY_EXEC 19.
- Per change, fast reader-only units range from 0 to 18. The largest is pr-5 with 18, then pr-22 with 13 and pr-24 with 7.

A spot check of pr-5 found two over-selecting shapes:

1. Suffix literals: `"_test.go"` in internal/obscorpus and internal/semescalate, and `".cem.json"` in internal/cem/wire.
2. Fixture paths written into temporary repositories: `docs/specs/REQUIREMENTS.tsv` in internal/sharedpath.

The same witness kind also covers real readers. Examples are a test that globs `*_test.go` in the
repository tree, and internal/scopelease and internal/evidencereach, which name
`cmd/corvint/main.go`. No static signal in the plan tells the two apart without a runtime read
trace. Decision: no narrowing. A zero-miss proof needs per-test observed reads, which the
selection-baseline replay does not record. The 4.8% fast-tier share bounds what such a proof could
save.
