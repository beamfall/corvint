# V1-1037 / V1-1041: procgroup ignores Darwin stop reports; containment tests prove their claims

Date: 2026-10-09
Tickets: V1-1037, V1-1041
Requirements: none changed. PGO-V0-008 already states that a stop or continue
report is not an exit for `internal/groupreap`; `internal/procgroup` (the
runner containment cited by `compat-trial-v0` and `CRR-V0-003`) now honours
the same rule.

## V1-1037 finding: confirmed by test

Base 986cec7c. `internal/procgroup`'s `waitProcessExitUnreaped` called
`waitid(P_PID, pid, WEXITED|WNOWAIT)` and returned success on any errno 0.
Darwin's waitid also returns for a stopped or continued child despite WEXITED
(golang/go#19314; the V1-0373 entry observed `si_code=5`, CLD_STOPPED, on
Darwin 25.6.0). A SIGSTOPped leader was therefore reported as exited and the
runner moved to group termination while the leader lived.
`TestWaitProcessExitUnreapedIgnoresStopAndContinue` fails on the base code
("returned for a stopped leader: <nil>") and passes now.

## Change

- `internal/procgroup/process_posix.go`: an errno-0 record whose `si_code` is
  CLD_TRAPPED, CLD_STOPPED or CLD_CONTINUED (4..6) is polled past every 5 ms
  (`processGroupPollInterval`) until a real exit, as `groupreap.leaderUnreaped`
  does. `waitidStateChangeOnly` mirrors `groupreap.stateChangeOnly` instead of
  importing it, so neither package gains a dependency or an exported helper.
- New `TestWaitProcessExitUnreapedIgnoresStopAndContinue` mirrors groupreap's
  `TestLeaderUnreapedIgnoresStopAndContinue` (stop, continue, kill) and also
  checks that the observed exit left the leader unreaped.
- Line citations of `internal/procgroup/process_posix.go` in `compat-trial-v0`
  and `compat-replay-runner-v0` move with the shifted lines; no requirement
  text changes.

## V1-1041 change

- `cmd/corvint/host_adapter_fail_open_test.go`: the slow Git shim sleeps
  `failOpenSlowGitSleep` (30 s, was 5 s) and the exit-kill bound is half the
  child's natural remaining lifetime, `(30 s - elapsed) / 2`, instead of a
  fixed 100 ms that also counted scheduler and reap latency. An unsignalled
  shim outlives the adapter by at least `30 s - elapsed`, so the bound still
  proves the exit kill with seconds of headroom (decision 0082).
- `internal/groupreap/live_unix_test.go`,
  `TestAHI048ConcurrentStartsReleasesAndKill`: workers now reap through the
  production `wait` (release, then reap) with the test registry installed as
  `liveGroups`. A barrier asserts all first starts are recorded at once; half
  of those first children stay blocked on stdin so the kill must signal live
  recorded groups (asserted), while the other half are released at the kill and
  keep starting and releasing until refused. 30 consecutive `-race` runs passed.

## Limits (recorded, not fixed)

- On Darwin a leader that stays stopped is now waited for, as it already was
  on Linux; the runner's timeout or cancellation still terminates it.
- Two other `SYS_WAITID` callers keep the unfixed classification and are out of
  this scope: `internal/dashboard/repository/process_wait_darwin.go`
  (`waitProcessExitUnreaped`) and `internal/testsupport/pipedrain_unix.go`.
- Linux runtime NOT_RUN on this host; Darwin arm64 only.
