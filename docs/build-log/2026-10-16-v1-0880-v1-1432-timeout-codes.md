## Test-first and mutation evidence

Each test was written before its fix and failed on the base `8e24ac26c`:

- **`TestV10880WorktreeChurnHasNamedCode`** had 5 of its 7 cases fail. The two
  `dogfood-event-unavailable` cases passed.
- **`TestV10880AdapterReasonNamesChurnAndKeepsUnavailable`** failed.
- **`TestAdapterDegradationAdmitsWorktreeChurn`** failed.
- **`TestMCPV0038CEMGitTimeoutHasDistinctCode`** failed in its `git_timeout` and `git_diff_timeout`
  cases.
- **`TestMCPV0038IndexDeadlineHasDistinctCode`** failed in its `index_deadline` case.
- **`TestEarlyExitReleaseDeadlineIsUnavailableNotDrift`** failed in its `stall` case.

Each fix was then broken once (the first mutations were killed):

- Churn mapped back to unavailable: 4 churn subtests failed.
- Unlisted errors mapped to churn: `unlisted_error` failed.
- `GitTimeout` restored to `repository-unavailable`: `git_timeout` failed.
- `indexDeadline` forced false: `index_deadline` failed.
- Early release seeded `deadlineOnly: false`: `stall` failed.
- Early release unavailable in every case: `stall-drift` and `fail` failed.
- The index-deadline check moved ahead of the cancellation check in `normalizeFailure` **survived**.
  This mutant is equivalent: `Registry.Call` re-checks `ctx.Err()` after the operation and returns
  `cancelled`, so caller precedence holds at the call boundary either way.

## Limits

- The docs bridge (`SDD-V0-011`) keeps its own `repository-index-timeout`, so the two MCP surfaces
  name the same class differently.
- `dogfood-event-worktree-churn` is not a time bound and does not join
  `conformance/host-lifecycle-v1`'s time-bound set.
- `make gate` is `NOT_RUN`, per the Verify section.
