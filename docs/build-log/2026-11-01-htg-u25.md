**V1-3860.**
- Reproduced the exact symptom on WSL with `unshare -rm` and TMPDIR on a noexec tmpfs:
  - the staged reset hook cannot exec;
  - the runs record `external-reset-failed`;
  - runner.log is absent.
- The same test passes on WSL and Unraid with exec-capable TMPDIRs: heavy.sh TMPDIR, /tmp, and the lane TMPDIR.
- The test now fails with this diagnosis:
  - `run repeat: reasons ["external-reset-failed"], hook reset failed`;
  - `hook.log not written ... the health hook never ran, so the reset step failed while staging or starting it`;
  - `staging probe in TMPDIR "/tmp": script did not run: fork/exec ...: permission denied`.
- That satisfies AC 1 (names the reset step and its cause) and AC 2 (the failure output carries the runner.log state).
