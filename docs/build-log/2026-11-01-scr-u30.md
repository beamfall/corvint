  - Fix: `ensureGoTmp` now writes telemetry mode `off` under a `closedGoEnv` home, and only for that

  - After the fix: `-count=100` passes, a `-race` run passes, and `go test -short

## Friction

1. `go` writes telemetry counters from a detached child after it exits, and `GOTELEMETRY=off` in the
   environment does not stop it.
   - Repro: `env -i HOME=$h GOTELEMETRY=off go env GOTMPDIR; sleep 1; find $h/.config`.
   - Expected: no files. Actual: a counter file, `upload.token` and `upload/`.
   - `internal/goplsclient`, `internal/lspprovider` and `internal/behaviorfalsify` pass
     `GOTELEMETRY=off` and may believe telemetry is off. They should be audited.
2. `scratch-sweep_test.sh` fails on a host without lsof.
   - Repro: `bash script/scratch-sweep_test.sh` on unraid.
   - Expected: pass, or a skip naming lsof. Actual: every entry is kept as in use, and the test fails
     on BASE.
3. The ticket list gave no per-ticket gap. Several tickets were assigned although they were already
   delivered on unmerged lanes (V1-3590, V1-3520, V1-3521 and V1-3619 on hst-u16 and htg-u25) or on
   main (V1-3519, V1-3533 and V1-3048), with no "In flight" note.
