# Forward epoch 2 after the dead-pin rows — V1-3314, V1-3309 AC3

Decision (orchestrator, under owner delegation; CIN-V0-001): advance `CORVINT_PR_FORWARD_EPOCH`,
`FORWARD_EPOCH` and the verdict artifact name to epoch 2 together. Since the 1.0 mint the tool and
shadow pins named old-history commit `b609ca4b`, absent from `beamfall/corvint` (V1-3309), so every
epoch-1 forward row was `INCOMPLETE`; five of them keep the AFP-V0-030 kill switch tripped even
after the repin (PR #11). AFP-V0-030 makes a reviewed `.github/` change advancing the epoch pin,
with a build-log entry naming the review, the only re-arm route. The advance clears the window
only: the AFP-V0-029 bar (20 complete rows, at least 5 failing, 0 `MISS`), the kill-switch
thresholds and the `MISS` rule are unchanged, no row is seeded, and narrowing stays FULL until 20
genuine epoch-2 rows exist.

V1-3309 AC3 (CIN-V0-002): the pin steps stay `continue-on-error` so the full suite still runs,
but a final shard step now fails with an annotation naming the pin, its value and whether the
fetch or the build failed. Owner-visible consequence: a dead pin is a red required check until
it is repinned, instead of a silent stream of `INCOMPLETE` rows.

Review finding (Codex P2 on c827217, orchestrator decision): the first cut relabelled rows with
the collector's `FORWARD_EPOCH`, so a run produced under epoch 1 but collected after the advance
would count as epoch 2. Fix: the producing run's shadow step stamps its own
`CORVINT_PR_FORWARD_EPOCH` as `forwardEpoch` into its selection document, and `collect.sh` names
the row with that value only. A missing, mixed, non-integer or other epoch, or no selection
document, writes no row, so the run is never counted, not even as `INCOMPLETE`. Owner-visible
trade-off: a shadow outage (no selection document) no longer counts `INCOMPLETE` toward the kill
switch; the bar still needs 20 complete epoch-2 rows, so narrowing stays FULL through it.
`FORWARD_EPOCH_SINCE` (2026-10-30T00:00:00Z) is now only a listing lower bound, not an evidence
boundary (decision (c)).

Evidence: `TestCINV0001EpochTwoLeavesTheDeadPinRowsBehind`, `TestCINV0002UnreachablePinFailsTheShardLoudly`,
the "producer epoch names the row" case of `TestAFPV0047CollectorNamesRows` (replays retained
artifacts under collector epoch 2: unstamped, epoch-1, string, fractional, negative and mixed
producers write no row; an epoch-2 producer writes `-e2-` rows; an epoch-2 producer under
collector 1 writes none), `TestAFPV0047SelectionDocument` (`tools/ci-forward-row`, PASS; whole
