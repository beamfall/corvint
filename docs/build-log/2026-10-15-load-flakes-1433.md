## Diagnosis

1. The session waits and the MCP read bound are hang detectors that load turned into performance
   budgets. The product timing those tests assert is already covered elsewhere:
   - stale classification is asserted from the session log;
   - process retirement keeps its own bounds;
   - MCP exit promptness keeps its 3 s checks.
2. STR-V0-015 makes the budget bound the whole shard. The test proves that by running two
   invocations that each fit the budget but together do not. Its margins were smaller than the
   discovery and launch cost under load. The product deadline (`run.go`, one deadline per shard) is
   correct.
3. The graceful test measured wall time from before `Execute` against a flat 8 s. That bound
   indirectly asserted the 5 s native-cleanup grace (`test-runner-execution-v0.md`, "at most five
   additional seconds"). On a loaded host the bound fired on a correct run.
   - Lifting the bound alone exposed a second race in the `interrupt` and `postbinding` modes. With
     a 1 s deadline, the deadline SIGINT can reach the helper before it installs its signal
     handling. The child then never starts, and the mode's assertions fail (`missing interruption`,
     `post-cleanup input mutation escaped binding`).

## Change

1. `session_test.go` adds `hangBound = 5 * time.Minute`, used for every event wait and for the
   fixture `Config.Timeout` (decision 0082; same value as the existing `runDeadline` and the
   cancellation fixture). Unchanged: the 3 s `assertGone`, the 10 s cancellation shutdown, and
   the 4 min child-PID wait.
2. `mcp_blackbox_test.go` adds `responseHangBound = 2 * time.Minute` for `readMessage`. The 3 s
   promptness checks are unchanged.
3. `runner_unix_test.go` raises the fake `go`'s slow invocation from 1 s to 7 s:
   - STR-V0-015 uses a 12 s shard budget. Each invocation fits under a 12 s per-invocation bound,
     but the pair (about 14 s) cannot fit a 12 s shard bound.
   - The STR-V0-014 budget case uses 10 s, so the grandchild starts before the kill.
   - PR #937 (V1-1311) adds a `now` clock seam to the runner. Once it lands, STR-V0-015 can move
     from wall-clock margins to the seam. Its hunks touch other regions of this file.
4. `execute_unix_test.go`, `TestGracefulInterruptLifecycle`:
   - The test now asserts the 5 s grace directly, as the graceful `cmd.WaitDelay` set by
     `containPhase`.
   - The wall-clock bound is `timeout + 29 s`, which separates the grace from the one-minute hard
     fallback.
   - A deadline-driven mode (`timeout`, `postbinding`, `uncooperative`) whose helper never started
     its child is inconclusive. It is retried with 1, 4 and then 16 s deadlines; the 16 s attempt
     is final and asserts.
   - `interrupt` is driven by readiness, so its deadline is a 60 s hang detector.
5. Not changed because not reproduced (refutation counts below):
   - `TestExecuteRetiresEscapedDetachedDescendants` (V1-1434);
   - the `cem/verify` S0E framing case (V1-1435);
   - `TestDiscriminateRecordsWitnessAndReportDowngrades` (V1-1390);
   - the `liveverify/mutate` package (V1-1439).
