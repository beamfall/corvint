# 2026-10-12 cem/0.x retirement, stage S2b: core 0.x removal (V1-1278)

Branch `claude/v1-1278-s2b-core-removal`, from `claude/v1-1278-s2-consumers` (`52af6ee1`, PR #851).
Decision 0525 §1 (only CEM 1.0) and §6 (superseded records). Requirements `CEM-V1-215..219`
(new, proposed) in `docs/specs/cem-stable-v1.md`; accepted requirements that still promised a 0.x
behaviour are amended in place with an amendment sentence (`CEM-V1-018`, `019`, `036`, `065`,
`210`, `212`; `FPK-V0-030`, `031`, `033`, `034`, `035`, `050`; `CF-V0-006..020`, `033`, `034`;
`CEM-CB-001..004`, `009..012`, `025`; `CEM-SM-006`, `018`; `CRT-V0-005`; `RCB-V0-006`;
`CEM-PILOT-009`; `CEM-V1-168`; `LOD-V0-009`, `LOD-V0-035`), under the owner's standing
auto-accept of lane requirements (2026-10-11). Also fixes V1-1326.

## Intent

S2a moved every consumer outside `internal/cem` to `cem/1.0`. S2b deletes what remained so the
product ships one wire: the `cem/0.1`..`cem/0.3` wire and verifier, the 0.x producers and `cem`
CLI routes, the frozen `frontier/0`, `tcq/0`, `lrf/0` and `external-frontier-obligations/0`
routes, the attest `cem/0` and `cem/v1` predicates and flags, the never-delivered dashboard
adapter `cem-ocm-bundle-v0`, and the 0.x branches of the dogfood and recipe scripts. The
`protocol/`, `interop/`, `examples/cem` and `conformance/cem-0.1` 0.x packets stay for S3.

## Decisions

- Kept mechanics. `wire.Map`, the structural and mechanical reasons, the coverage and
  discrimination witnesses, `internal/cem/patch` and `canonicalChange` in
  `internal/cem/verify/verify.go` stay: the stable reader, producer, verifier and
  `verify/candidate.go` use them. `MechanicalReason` lost its spec argument; structural reasons
  are always admitted (the `cem/0.3` meaning `cem/1.0` inherits). `ParseMap`, `ParseMapSpec`,
  `Canonical`, `Spec01..03`, `ReadOptions.SingleSpec`, `verify.Exact`, `verify.Canonical` and
  `internal/cem/wire/route.go` are deleted.
- Producers. `cem begin`/`prepare` default to and accept only `cem/1.0`; a retired label refuses
  with `unsupported-spec` plus `wire.RetiredSpecFix`, anything else with `invalid-arguments`
  (`CEM-V1-216`). The `cem` command parser no longer lists `--spec` choices, so a retired label
  reaches that producer refusal instead of an argparse `invalid choice`
  (`TestCEMV1216CommandSpecRefusals`). `internal/cem/stablefixture` builds from the real
  `cem/1.0` producer only.
- Reads. `cem status`, `verify`, `report` and `mark` refuse every root that is not exactly
  `cem/1.0` before any Git read (`CEM-V1-215`).
- `frontier/0`. The engine (`types`, `upstream`, `compute`, `closure`, `document`, `identity`,
  `verify` and their tests) is deleted; `internal/frontier/stable.go` stays as the named
  successor `frontier-stable-view/0` (V1-1304) and `profile.go` holds the surviving constants.
  The CLI refuses a map that does not declare `cem/1.0` with `unsupported-spec` in the closed
  CF-V0-022 envelope. `conformance/frontier-v0` retires its whole fixture tier (30 fixtures, 84
  cases), not only the 26 S2a skips, because no case can bind without the engine: the
  manifest's `retired` record lists every fixture, matrix row and case with its reason, and the
  codec and identity vectors stay (`TestManifestPinsRetirementAndArtifacts`,
  `TestArtifactDigestDriftFails`). The shared states vocabulary test now checks `ocm-v0` only.
- `tcq/0`. `Evaluate`, the `tcq/0` upstream verifier and the oracle document file are deleted.
  The shared helpers `EvaluateStable` uses stay. The oracle's inputs and section expectations are
  ported to `testdata/stable-fixture.json` over a `cem/1.0` OCM binding
  (`TestStableSectionsMatchOracle`, `TestCacheVerificationAcceptsComputedBytes`); the failure,
  flake and seam tests run through `EvaluateStable`.
- `external-frontier-obligations/0`. `Obligations` and its schema are deleted; the impact join is
  kept as `obligations_join.go`; the CLI always calls `StableObligations`, which refuses the
  retired frozen route.
- `lrf/0`. `conformance/lrf-v0/cases.json` is replaced by `retired.json` (41 cases, each with
  its action and manifest digest). `manifest.json` and `registration.json` stay byte-frozen
  because the registration binds the manifest digest. The DTO and observe harness and the
  corpus test are deleted. `lrf evaluate` already refused every map (S2a).
- Attestation. `internal/attest/cem_v1.go` and the v0 builder are deleted; `cem.go` keeps the
  retired-predicate table and the shared claim check. A `cem/0` or `cem/v1` envelope fails with
  "predicateType ... is retired; cem/v2 is the only CEM predicate" (CLI
  `attest-verification-failed`), and `--attest-cem`/`--attest-cem-v1` are unrecognized arguments
  (`CEM-V1-218`).
- Freezes under decision 0525 §6 (`CEM-V1-219`). The core freeze's `cem status`/`cem verify`
  goldens and `cmd/corvint/testdata/cem-status-canonical.golden` are regenerated from a `cem/1.0`
  map read with `--artifacts` (`CORVINT_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`); decision 0357's freeze of the 0.x
  goldens is superseded. cli-parity retires `cem-begin-map` (`retiredCaseDecisions`, manifest
  `retired` reason), so replay prints `SUMMARY parity=102 retired=31`.
- Dashboard. The declared, never-delivered `cem-ocm-bundle-v0` adapter is removed: the registry
  goes from 11 to 10 rows and its digests (`ExpectedAdapterRegistrySHA256`, the typed registry
  digest and the dashboard-snapshot-v0 vectors' `adapterRegistrySha256`/`snapshotSha256`) are
  recomputed (`LOD-V0-009`, `LOD-V0-035` amended).
- Scripts. `script/cem-verify-pr_test.sh` and `script/verify-receipt-bundle.sh` with its test are
  deleted (Makefile, gate-ledger, unbounded-readers, runtime-environment test and INDEX updated;
  `RCB-V0-006`, `CEM-PILOT-009` amended). `script/cem-recipes_test.sh` drops its 0.x recipes.
  `script/dogfood-bind-range.sh` reads only the compact `cem/1.0` sidecar line and always passes
  `--artifacts`; its fakes and `script/dogfood-change_test.sh` use compact `cem/1.0` sidecars.
- V1-1326. `internal/dogfoodflow/check.go` read the previous sidecar's base with a regular
  expression that matched only the 0.x indented layout. `sidecarBaseRevision` now walks the
  top-level JSON object (a repeated, missing, nested-only, trailing or non-object member gives
  no base), so a compact `cem/1.0` sidecar binds its base (`TestSidecarBaseRevisionReadsAnyLayout`).
- Tests (`CEM-V1-214`). A test that guarded behaviour `cem/1.0` keeps was ported to the stable
  producer or `stablefixture`; a 0.x-only test was deleted; a refusal test that needs a retired
  map relabels producer bytes to `cem/0.2` (refusal only, never the other direction). The
  `UC-EVIDENCE-CARRYING-COMPLETION` hostile cases (`TestUseCaseHostileEvidenceCompletion`) and
  the promisor-clone refusal (`TestMapCoreVerbsRefuseAPromisorObjectWithoutFetching`) now prepare
  a `cem/1.0` map and read it with a full target commit and `--artifacts`; every case keeps its
  refusal code and exit, and a rejected read reports `state: rejected` instead of `invalid`.
  `TestCEMLegacyReadKeepsResolvedRoot` is deleted: it pinned root handling for a 0.x read that
  now refuses before the root matters, and `TestCEMStableReadKeepsCallerRoot` keeps the
  stable root spellings.
- Line citations. Deleting and editing code moved pinned `path:line@anchor` citations in
  `docs/DOGFOOD.md`, decisions 0051, 0165 and 0173, the frontier decision brief and the specs. A citation whose bytes moved
  unchanged is repinned to its new line with the same anchor; three (the `prove_attest_cem.go`
  drift bracket, the `attest-failed` site and `frontier_adapters.go`) were reread and repinned
  with new anchors. Citations into deleted files (`internal/frontier/{types,closure}.go`,
  `conformance/frontier-v0/{capabilities,fixtures_test}.go`, `conformance/lrf-v0/cases.json`)
  are rewritten as prose naming the file and line at commit `52af6ee1`.

## Failure modes

- A reintroduced 0.x parser or verifier call fails acceptance search 1 below.
- A 0.x map handed to a Core read or producer refuses with `unsupported-spec` and the recovery
  line, never an internal error or a fallback.
- A retired attestation envelope fails closed; the removed flags are argument errors.
- Editing a retired frozen tier without its retirement record fails its manifest test.
