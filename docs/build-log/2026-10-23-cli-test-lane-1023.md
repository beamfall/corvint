## V1-2305: cli tests orphan dispatch workers (ATR-V0-310)

Cause: the dead-man reaper of V1-2189 hooks `dispatch.launch` from inside the dispatch package's own
test binary (an unexported seam), so a test binary of another package that starts the dispatcher
has nothing. Two `host.sh` workers of an `internal/tasks/cli` test survived 9 to 12 hours.

Decision: do not widen the dispatch seam. The cli test binary finds the groups the dispatcher starts
by their shape (a group leader whose parent is the binary) and signals only what it can pin. The
design after the independent review (section "Review repair" below) has three layers
(`worker_reap_internal_test.go`, `main_internal_test.go`):

- `ReapWorkersOnCleanup(t)` (per test, run last, also after `t.Fatal`) fails the test naming every
  group led by a child of the binary that was not running when it was called, and retires those it
  owns. It is called by the tests that start dispatcher workers:
  `TestCALV0052_DispatchCLIClaimHandoffAndStatus`,
  `TestATRV0101_WorkerWithoutDispatcherVerbReceivesNoticesThroughHook`,
  `TestCALV0053_DispatchReaderCLICrashRetainsQuarantine` and both `TestPSRDispatchNativeAdapter` cases.
- `TestMain` joins the detached children the tests launched (bounded, no signal), then retires any
  such group left after the last test and fails the package.
- A dead-man reaper (the binary re-executed as the leader of its own process group, held unreaped
  by the binary through `groupreap.Owner`; standard input a pipe only the binary holds) samples the
  process table every 200 ms and, at end of file, retires what it owns and exits nonzero with a
  report on the binary's standard error. This covers SIGKILL and the package timeout, when no
  cleanup runs. A binary that tests re-execute as a helper inherits `CORVINT_CLI_TEST_ROOT` and
  stays out of the reaping.

Ownership (all three layers, `reapTracker`): the process table is `groupreap.ReadProcessTable`
(new, read-only: sysctl `kern.proc.all` on Darwin, `/proc/<pid>/stat` on Linux; each row's pid,
parent, group and start time come from one read, and any failure other than a vanished process is
an error). A process is attributed only from a row read after its handle is opened, the handle
being found still pinned after the read. On Linux the handle is a pidfd, which the kernel keeps
naming the process it was opened on and which reports `ESRCH` once that process is reaped, so a
signal through it never reaches a reused pid. Retirement stops an owned leader, kills the owned
members through their pidfds while the stopped leader pins the group number, samples again for
members forked meanwhile, and kills the leader last. No signal is ever sent to a numeric pid or
group. Darwin has no handle a non-parent can signal through without a reuse window, so there the
layers attribute and report but signal nothing: a leak fails the test, the package or the reaper
run and is left running.

Limit: a group started and ended within one 200 ms sample of the binary's death is not attributed
by the reaper; the per-test and end-of-run checks do not sample. On Darwin a leak is reported and
not killed.

Evidence: `TestATRV0310_LeakedWorkerGroupsAreReaped` re-executes the test binary as a helper that
starts a real dispatcher worker (`dispatch --once`, a host whose shell and background subshell run
until the test removes a run file, so the test itself never signals it) and then (1) leaks it after
registering `ReapWorkersOnCleanup`: the helper must fail naming the group; (2) leaks it
unregistered: the package must fail; (3) is SIGKILLed after the reaper logged the group: the
reaper must report it. On Linux each group must also be gone within 30 s (killed: the helper
reported `killed [50 51 52 53]`, the leader, subshell and two sleeps); on Darwin it must be named
"not signalled, ownership not proven" and still be running, after which the run file's removal
ends it. With the three layers disabled (`TestMain` not arming, `ReapWorkersOnCleanup` a no-op)
all three subtests fail (first round, before the review).

## V1-2173: detached equals foreground (ATR-V0-312)

The V1-1557 decision (`2026-10-21-detach-claim-semantics-and-prune.md`) made `--detach` a transport
with no semantics of its own. `TestATRV0312_DetachedClaimOutcomeEqualsForeground` builds two stores the
same way, gives holder `agent` a live attempt under request id `r1`, and sends three claims in the
foreground to one store and as `--detach --wait 30` to the other: another ticket under `r2` (a second
attempt, generation 2), the held ticket under `r3` (`ATTEMPT_LIVE`) and the held ticket under `r1`
(idempotent replay). The exit code and envelope must be equal after removing the random attempt id,
wall-clock instants and the digests of records that carry them, and the three outcomes must differ.
Mutant (reverted): the detached child rewriting `ATTEMPT_LIVE` fails it.

## V1-1559: Linux detach evidence and the LOST attach race (ATR-V0-311)

Linux (first round, cloud host): the 25 detach tests (`ATRV0021..0029`, `ATRV0050`, `0053`, `0066`,
`0095`, `V11721`, `PSRV0111..0114`, `PSRV0185`) plus the new ones passed, 28.7 s.
Race: `leaseDetachAwait` read the child's liveness first and the launch lock second. The launcher
writes `child.json` and then releases the lock, so a launch that completed between the two reads
left "no child on record" and "lock free", and the attach reported `LOST` for a child that was
running. The first fix read the lock, liveness, the lock again and liveness again; the
independent review showed it still answered `LOST` when a retry took the lock after the second
lock read and paused before recording its child. The attach now takes the launch lock shared,
without waiting, before its liveness read and holds it until it has chosen its answer
(`leaseDetachShareLaunch`): no launch can begin during the decision, and a launcher already
holding the lock makes the answer `PENDING`. The shared lock is the same brief `LOCK_SH|LOCK_NB`
the old probe took, so a launcher's polling wait is held up by at most one read, as before.

Tests: `TestATRV0311_AttachNeverReportsALaunchCompletingBetweenItsReadsLost` completes a launch
inside the `leaseDetachProbeGap` seam and requires `PENDING`; it fails with the original
liveness-first order (regression witness). `TestATRV0311_LateRelaunchIsNeverReportedLost` drives
the review's schedule through the identity probe, which every liveness read calls: a retry tries
the launch lock at the second liveness read (case 1) or at every one (case 2) and, holding it,
pauses before recording its child; an attach that answered `LOST` while the retry held the lock
fails it. Case 1 fails on the first-round code (`SUPERVISOR_LOST` after two liveness reads,
checked); on the current code the retry is refused the lock while the attach decides (case 2)
and `LOST` is then correct. `TestATRV0311_AttachDuringARelaunchIsPending` is a state check
(coverage addition): a held lock gives `PENDING`, a released one `LOST`.
`TestATRV0311_AttachDuringChildStartupIsNeverLost` attaches continuously through a real launch
with the injected 1.5 s child startup delay; it also passes on the old order (the window is too
narrow to hit reliably), so it is end-to-end coverage, not a regression witness.

## Review repair (2026-10-23, round 3)

The independent review blocked the reaper on process ownership and found the late-relaunch
schedule above. Each code finding has a test; failures before the repair were shown on this
macOS host (Darwin 25, arm64, `go1.27.3`) by the named mutant of the repaired code, which
restores the reviewed behaviour, or by the reviewed code itself:

| finding | test | shown failing by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 group signal by number after the group may be reused | `TestATRV0310_NoSignalWithoutAPinnedOwner` | mutant accepting a process on group-number presence alone: signals `100/9`, `301/10` (a stranger under the reused number) |
| 2 membership from an earlier snapshot | `TestATRV0310_MembershipIsReadFromThePinnedProcess` | mutant taking membership from the pre-handle snapshot: the replacement of pid 200 attributed |
| 3 late relaunch reported `LOST` | `TestATRV0311_LateRelaunchIsNeverReportedLost` | the reviewed `lease_detach.go`: `SUPERVISOR_LOST` after two liveness reads while the retry held the lock |
| 4 inventory failure or `EPERM` taken for gone | `TestATRV0310_UnobservableCleanupFails` | mutants: a failed table read returning nothing (reaper exit 0 after a failed sample); `EPERM` taken as gone |
| 5 reused worker pid never tracked | `TestATRV0310_ReusedWorkerPIDIsTrackedByIncarnation` | mutant keeping reaped incarnations: the later worker under pid 100 not tracked |
| 6 stalled reaper and its child left running | `TestATRV0310_StalledReaperIsRetiredWithItsGroup` | the reviewed shutdown (kill the reaper only, no wait), in a scratch copy: the reaper's child outlived it |
| 7 detached child still exiting at the leak check | `TestATRV0310_DetachedChildrenAreJoinedBeforeTheLeakCheck` | the joins removed: the helper package failed "tests left worker process groups running" |

Finding 7: a detached result is readable before its child exits. A nil-outside-tests seam
`detachLaunched` (in `lease_detach.go`, also called by `attempt_detach.go`) hands each launched
child's reap channel to the test binary; `detachClaimStore` registers `JoinDetachedChildren`, which
waits, bounded at 60 s and without signalling, for the children launched under its store before
the test's cleanup ends, and `TestMain` joins every remaining one before the strict leak check.
The helper's detached child stays 1.5 s past its kept result (`CORVINT_TEST_LEASE_DETACH_EXIT_MS`,
read only by the test helper).

Finding 6: the reaper no longer starts a process (`ps` is replaced by the sysctl table); it is
started through `groupreap.Start`, and shutdown waits up to the bound, then kills its whole group
while its leader is still unreaped, and reaps it; a reaper that failed or was killed fails the
package.

Finding 8: the acceptance evidence in the spec now separates regression witnesses, mutant checks
and coverage additions. `TestATRV0312_DetachedClaimOutcomeEqualsForeground` and
`TestCALV0350_ClaimNextDerivesScopeForThePreviewedWaiterCLI` cover existing behaviour and pass on
the previous implementation; their mutants are named in their sections above.
