## Decision

Core consumes only the Tasks wire package. Planning and historical verification invoke an independently supplied absolute native Tasks executable with its independently supplied SHA256. Native read commands capture a coherent audited ticket, attempt and policy, and verify retained captures. Historical verification launches no experiment or test executable. Transport has fixed arguments, a closed environment, bounded output, a deadline and process-group cleanup.

The retained claim-era canonical ticket bytes must match the attempt ticket-record digest. Ordered criteria and acceptance revision must remain continuous; a body-only ticket edit is permitted. Attempt and lease sequence numbers cannot exceed the captured audit head. Older experimental captures without claim-era bytes are unsupported by the new verifier; preserve their prior reader and artifacts.
