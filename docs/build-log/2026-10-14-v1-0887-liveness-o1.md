# V1-0887 liveness write cost against history (issue 642)

Date: 2026-10-14. Base: `b675a948191bf5b48036b1910bcde8454b82bdf0`.
Requirement: `CAL-V0-224` (`docs/specs/corvint-tasks-agent-leases-v0.md`), accepted under the
owner's standing auto-accept (2026-10-11) (V1-0887). No decision file: liveness records stay in the
audited journal, so acceptance criterion 3 does not apply. 1.0 scope: `PRS-V1-018`; this ticket's
milestone is post-1-0.

## Intent

Native ticket V1-0887 (GitHub issue 642, closed) asks that a heartbeat or renew that changes
nothing else complete in milliseconds on a store of at least 13,000 receipts, with cost flat from
3,000 to 13,000 receipts, while lease, generation fencing and refusal semantics stay unchanged.

## Measurement at base

`TestCALV0118_WriterHoldProfile` (`internal/tasks/store`, opt-in), built from the base, 880
tickets, synthetic stores under the lane scratch directory, median of five per verb. Each series
records the one-minute load average before and after it. Wall time is in milliseconds; the phases
are the `CAL-V0-116` writer stages reported by `--timing`.

| Receipts | Load (1 min) | Heartbeat | Observe | Model | Bind | Commit | Renew | Observe |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3,000 | 9.84 to 11.22 | 417.6 | 68.2 | 198.2 | 25.2 | 109.7 | 413.8 | 62.8 |
| 8,000 | 14.10 to 13.11 | 441.2 | 85.6 | 184.9 | 36.3 | 118.5 | 445.9 | 91.0 |
| 13,000 | 12.68 to 13.61 | 457.2 | 104.7 | 197.6 | 30.6 | 110.0 | 440.5 | 90.2 |

Raw heartbeat samples at 13,000: 457.2, 440.2, 456.2, 466.8 and 1,013.5 ms. The checkpoint read
stays at 1 to 2 ms. A heartbeat therefore costs hundreds of milliseconds, not milliseconds, and
grows about 9 % from 3,000 to 13,000 receipts. Only the observe stage grows.

An in-process profile of 40 `observeWriter` calls (mean 60.4 ms at 3,000 receipts, 106.1 ms at
13,000) attributes about 640 of the 790 ms CPU difference to `scanReceiptNames`, the `receipts/`
listing that each of the writer audit's two captures performs. The rest is heap growth for the
name slices. Writer checkpoint decoding is not significant. The audit reads only the checkpoint's
receipt and the tail.

## Decision

No behaviour change. `CAL-V0-224` records the characterization and adds a maintained guard,
`TestCALV0224_WriterAuditWorkIsHistoryIndependent`. At 4 and 48 history receipts with a tail of
3, it counts `receipts/` listings and receipt-file reads per settled writer audit (both the
`AuditForWrite` and the `AuditForMutation` selections) and requires 2 listings and 4 reads. It
then requires a temporary receipt name that appears during the walk to decline the fast route.
It asserts no duration. A scratch mutation that read half the prefix receipts in `walkWriter` made
it fail with 8 and 30 reads; the scratch was reverted.

Options considered and rejected:

1. Probing for the next receipt name instead of listing makes observe O(1). However, it misses a
   missing, stray or crash-left temporary receipt name, so a fast write would commit where the
   complete route refuses (for example "active staging recovery is not implemented"). That changes
   refusal semantics (acceptance criterion 2) and the accepted `CAL-V0-116` exact 1..head rule.
2. Reusing the first capture's listing in the second capture while `receipts/` keeps the same
   identity, mode, size and modification time, mirroring `CAL-V0-189`, was implemented. Its guard
   passed and failed without the reuse, but it was withdrawn after the review (finding 1 below).
   A non-cooperating name change that keeps the directory stamps, under a coarse or restored
   modification time, would go unseen during the walk. Unlike `CAL-V0-189` intent bytes, there is
   no content recheck before effects. Its gain was about half of the observe stage's growth
   (profile estimate). A post-change series at 3,000 receipts ran at load 50 to 70, with a
   2,534 ms heartbeat median, so it is not evidence and was discarded.
3. Moving liveness out of the journal. This is the last resort in the charter, needs a decision
   (criterion 3), and is not needed to keep fencing exact.

## Acceptance

1. Residual. A heartbeat or renew costs about 0.42 to 0.46 s and grows about 9 % (40 ms) from
   3,000 to 13,000 receipts. The growth is the two exact `receipts/` listings that `CAL-V0-116`
   requires. Most of the cost is ticket-proportional (model about 190 ms, commit about 110 ms),
   which V1-0915 already recorded. The write that crosses the 512-receipt refresh interval still
   pays a complete audit after it releases the lock (V1-0916, open).
2. Met, with no behaviour change. The existing writer fast-route, fencing and refusal suites pass,
   and the new guard covers the exact listing and the declined fast route.
3. Not applicable. Liveness stays in the audited journal.

## Review

One read-only Codex round over the uncommitted diff against the base.

1. Blocker, confirmed: the listing reuse (option 2) could hide a name change with unchanged
   stamps. The reuse was reverted.
2. Minor, confirmed: the test comment overstated the result ("receipt work does not grow"), since
   the listing still reads every name. Rewritten to claim reads only.
3. Minor: this build log was not yet written when the review ran. It is now.

## Limits

The base series ran under a host load of 10 to 14. The post-change series ran under 50 to 70 and is
discarded. With no behaviour change, the base series is the retained measurement. Darwin only.
Linux readdir cost NOT_RUN. Live fleet heartbeat qualification NOT_RUN.

## Rollback

Delete the test, this entry and the `CAL-V0-224` amendment. No code, store, receipt or wire format
depends on them.
