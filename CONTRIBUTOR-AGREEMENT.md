This agreement preserves contributors' ownership while allowing Corvint to distribute
their contributions under its public licenses and under separate commercial terms. It is not a
copyright assignment. It does not change the license of an existing Corvint release.

Read these terms before accepting. They permit proprietary commercial licensing of your covered
contributions without further payment to you. Submitting a pull request or signing a DCO alone
does not accept these additional terms. Section 5 provides a manual acceptance process; no
external signing service or paid legal review is required.

## 1. Parties and contribution scope

The contributor is the individual or organization identified in an acceptance record. Someone
accepting for an organization must have authority to bind that organization. Russell Lewis is the
recipient. Corvint is a project name, not a separate contracting company. The project repository
is [beamfall/corvint](https://github.com/beamfall/corvint).

Each acceptance must identify the agreement version and immutable Git revision containing its
text, the Corvint repository, and the exact contribution commits it covers. Only material
deliberately identified in that record is a contribution under this agreement. Issue discussion,
bug reports, unrelated work, later commits, and separately identified third-party material are
excluded unless expressly covered by an additional acceptance and sufficient rights.

## 5. Acceptance and records

An acceptance covers only the identified contribution commits and agreement text revision. It
becomes effective when the contributor makes the express acceptance below and Russell Lewis, or
a maintainer expressly authorized by him to accept these agreements, acknowledges acceptance for
the project. Acceptance and contributor authority must be verified before merge.

For a pull request in the project repository, use the authenticated GitHub account associated with
your contribution to post the statement below, replacing every placeholder. The agreement revision
must be the full Git commit ID of the version you read; link to that exact file revision. List all
covered contribution commits as full IDs. Each rights holder must provide its own sufficient grant.

```text
I, [full name], GitHub @[account], accept the Corvint contributor license agreement
version 1.0 at [immutable CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md URL and full commit ID] for
contribution commits [full commit IDs] in https://github.com/beamfall/corvint.
I act [for myself / as an authorized representative of: full organization name, role].
I have authority to grant its copyright, patent, and other permissions, including
commercial sublicensing, for this contribution. I have disclosed any third-party
material and restrictions. I intend this authenticated statement as my electronic
signature and acceptance of those terms.
```

The accepting maintainer replies with the accepted agreement revision and contribution IDs, then
retains a dated copy of both statements and their URLs/account identities in owner-controlled
private rights records. Retain the exact agreement text and accepted contribution bytes, not just
mutable comment links. Missing identity, uncertain authority, third-party restrictions, or mismatched
commits keep the contribution on hold until resolved; a comment alone does not establish ownership.

New contribution commits require coverage before merge. For squash, rebase, or other rewriting,
retain the accepted-to-merged commit mapping and verify that the accepted content is preserved.
Conflict-resolution edits or other additions need their own sufficient rights and acceptance where
required; a mapping cannot grant rights to new content.

The public statement intentionally identifies its signer and any represented organization. Do not
post home addresses, identity documents, employment contracts, or other sensitive evidence. A
contributor who needs private acceptance may request a private channel from the owner without
posting those details; that contribution waits until the owner and contributor agree on a channel
and the owner retains an equivalent authenticated written acceptance and acknowledgment.

The recipient will use private acceptance and authority records only to administer or establish
contribution rights, limit access to people performing that function, and retain them while those
rights are relied upon and for any further legally required period. Do not commit those records
to the repository. A project successor receiving them must assume these obligations. Existing
public GitHub statements remain subject to GitHub's hosting and retention practices.

Earlier submissions receive no retroactive permission merely because this agreement is published.
Their rights holders must separately accept for the identified work, or that work remains under
its existing terms and must be excluded or replaced where alternative licensing needs more rights.
